Content & copyright

Report text, translation, recording or rights concerns to islamicappsdeveloper@gmail.com.

Report a correction

Include the surah and ayah, dua title or ID, recording name, or mosque and timetable date. Tell us what appears incorrect and provide the source you are comparing it with. A timestamp is especially useful for an audio issue. Please avoid sending full copyrighted works when a reference or short identifying excerpt is sufficient.

Report a rights concern

Identify the work, its owner, your relationship to the owner, where it appears in DeenHub, and the use you are concerned about. Include a way to contact you and any relevant permission or licence information. Please do not send identity documents or sensitive information unless specifically needed later.

We will review the identified material and may restrict availability while investigating. Content-availability updates can remove access without a new app release once a compatible device receives them. This does not guarantee instant removal from offline devices, operating-system displays or copies outside DeenHub.

Sources and permissions

Names and source credits identify material; they do not by themselves establish permission for every use. Each work retains its applicable licence and ownership. A successfully downloaded or decoded recording is not a certification that its recitation matches the displayed text.

For information about the details you send, see our report privacy information.
