DeenHub support

Open the DeenHub support form, or contact islamicappsdeveloper@gmail.com. Include “DeenHub” and the feature in your subject.

For a bug, include your app version/build, device and OS version, the steps you took and what happened. Share only the screenshots and personal information needed to explain it.

Prayer times & location

My prayer times differ from my masjid

Check your selected city, time zone, calculation method, Asr setting and any adjustments. Calculated prayer starts and congregation times (iqamah) are different. Check the mosque schedule’s source and date; missing or stale times are not reliable current times.

How do I change my city?

Open the city selector from the city on Home and confirm your selection. Optional travel suggestions require location permission and your confirmation. iOS controls background location events, so a city-boundary alert is not immediate or guaranteed.

An Adhan or reminder did not sound

Check global and per-prayer choices, notification permission, Focus and sound settings. Full Adhan plays in the foreground or after tapping its notification; background Adhan uses a system alert sound. Open DeenHub periodically to renew reminders. A saved preference is not proof that iOS delivered an alert.

Reading & content

Can I read offline?

Qur’an text is available after a successful download. Audio is downloaded separately. Dua text is bundled; some recordings are unavailable pending review. Optional source resources can expire or be withdrawn.

What do Mushaf pages mean?

Optional admitted edition boundaries group complete ayahs. Text adapts to your screen; the app does not reproduce printed line breaks or a printed font facsimile. Plain IndoPak is separate from supported Uthmani colour Tajweed; coloured IndoPak annotations are not available.

How do Al-Kahf reminders work?

The catch-up reminder uses Friday Asr in your selected prayer city. An earlier reminder is optional. Eligible visible Arabic reading contributes to local coverage; covering the full surah marks that Friday complete. You can mark it read if you read elsewhere. Listening, seeking or opening alone does not complete it. Turning reminders off retains saved progress. iOS permissions and delivery settings still apply.

For a text, recording or rights concern, use our content-reporting guide.

Your information

Manage permissions in iOS Settings. Individual notes, bookmarks, sets and prayer records have their own controls. App removal does not guarantee iCloud, Watch or submitted-report deletion. For a submitted report, send us its receipt or enough detail to identify it. See the privacy policy.

Giving & supporting the app

Charitable payments happen on the selected charity’s website; contact the charity about recurring gifts, receipts or refunds. A DeenHub giving reminder never charges you.

Optional one-time tips and monthly support for DeenHub are being prepared. They are not yet available in the current build and will be separate from charity donations. Monthly support will renew until cancelled in Apple’s subscription settings. When available, use Apple’s checkout and Apple’s purchase support for purchase or refund questions.
