Using the app

DeenHub provides tools for prayer planning, Qur’an reading, supplications, reminders and personal records. Check the selected city, calculation settings and content sources. Mosque times depend on their sources and freshness. Compass readings depend on device conditions. Illustrative artwork is not astronomical tracking, and reading coverage is not proof of comprehension or worship.

Respect others’ rights when submitting reports or timetable material. Do not include private information about other people without permission. Submissions are reviewed; sending a timetable does not make it an approved schedule.

Tips and charity donations

Optional one-time app-support tips and monthly support are planned but not yet available in the current build. When offered, they will be voluntary App Store purchases supporting DeenHub’s development. The monthly option will be an auto-renewing subscription charged each month until you cancel in your Apple subscription settings; one-time tips will be charged once. They will not unlock features, buy religious standing or represent a charitable donation or tax receipt. The App Store will show the applicable local price and purchase or renewal terms before you authorize payment. You will be able to manage your subscription through Apple; restoration will apply to an eligible subscription, not to consumed tips. We do not treat cancellation or pending approval as payment success.

Charitable giving is separate: an external link opens a charity’s website, where you choose the amount and authorize any one-time or recurring agreement. The charity manages payments, receipts, cancellation and refunds. DeenHub’s giving reminder does not authorize payment or confirm a donation.
